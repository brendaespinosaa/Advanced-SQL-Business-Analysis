# ⚙️ Advanced SQL Business Analysis

## Overview

This project explores a real-world Brazilian e-commerce dataset using advanced SQL techniques to solve business problems and generate strategic business insights.

The analysis focuses on customer behavior, revenue performance, retention, churn risk, seasonality, and marketplace operations. The project simulates real-world business intelligence and analytics scenarios commonly found in data-driven companies.

The objective was not only to perform SQL queries, but to build a complete business analytics project with professional documentation, structured analysis, and actionable insights.

---

## Business Problem

E-commerce and marketplace companies often face challenges such as:

- Difficulty identifying high-value customers
- Lack of visibility into customer retention behavior
- Limited understanding of churn risk
- Poor visibility into seasonality and revenue trends
- Difficulty measuring customer lifetime value
- Limited operational insights regarding logistics and delivery performance

Without proper analytical visibility, decision-making becomes reactive instead of strategic.

---

## Solution

This project acts as a complete SQL-based business intelligence analysis environment, transforming raw marketplace data into actionable business insights.

The analysis includes:

- Revenue analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Customer lifetime value analysis
- Churn risk classification
- Cohort retention analysis
- Marketplace performance evaluation
- Operational and logistics analysis

The project was structured to simulate real analytics workflows used by BI and Data Analytics teams.
